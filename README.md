# DATA200---Final-Project
Introduction: When proffesional baseball teams are putting together their opening day 26-man roster they have many desisions to make. One of their hardest decision is to decide between rostering a reliable veteran or a high-upside young player. The veteran will normally give you consistent numbers while the young player could waste a roster spot or become a star player after a breakout. MLB teams are turning to the numbers in order to determine if a young player will breakout. In this study we will be using the metric wOBA which is calculated by; wOBA = (0.690×uBB + 0.722×HBP + 0.888×1B + 1.271×2B + 1.616×3B + 2.101×HR) / (AB + BB – IBB + SF + HBP). The advanced metric wOBA measures a player's overall offensive contribution per plate apperance by assigning a proper value for each type of hitting event. We will being using wOBA to try and predict breakout players per year then validating it on 2024 data and testing it on 2025 data.
